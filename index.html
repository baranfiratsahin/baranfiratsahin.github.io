Baran Fırat Şahin is an offensive security specialist and independent systems builder based in Türkiye. His work spans authorized penetration testing, vulnerability assessment and system hardening; fullstack and edge engineering with real-time interfaces and live data visualization; applied AI, including fully offline local language models, retrieval systems and autonomous agent architectures; systematic market infrastructure with risk control and validation discipline; large-scale archive and corpus processing; e-commerce operations; 3D production pipelines; and desktop and process automation. He runs IT operations at Fırat Group / BEQW Systems and builds and ships production systems himself — some public, some kept private for operational security or commercial confidentiality.
